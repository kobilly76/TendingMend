Our policies

Clear rules, so there are no surprises.

🪪 ID required

A valid ID is required to drop off a repair and to pick up your device.

✅ No fix, no pay

If we can’t fix it, there’s no repair charge. If we performed diagnostics before determining it can’t be fixed, you pay the diagnostic fee only.

🎓 Student discount

Students get $5 off. Bring your student ID.

💾 Data loss

Tech Mend isn’t liable for lost or missing data unless you purchased a data backup or transfer from us. We strongly recommend backing up before any repair.

🔩 Special-order parts

If a repair needs a part we have to order, 50% payment is required upfront so we’re covered if the order falls through.

🔋 Hazardous batteries

Swollen, leaking, or damaged batteries are a safety risk. An extra fee applies for safe disposal and/or replacement.

📦 Abandoned devices

Once your device is ready for pick-up and it isn’t collected, it’s considered abandoned and becomes the property of Tech Mend, which may be recycled or reused. We’ll notify you before this happens.

🚫 Stolen devices

We refuse service on stolen devices and will report them to the authorities.

⚠️ Note: We don’t offer consultations. Drop your device off and we’ll take it from there.
